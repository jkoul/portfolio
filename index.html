03 / About

A different path to engineering.

I've always been fascinated by finding meaningful patterns in noisy systems. That curiosity first drew me toward data, economics, public policy, and research—and eventually toward building the technology myself.

My career has crossed grassroots advocacy, nonprofit research, strategic communications, civic technology, and software engineering. The connecting question has been remarkably consistent: how can we help ordinary people better understand and shape the decisions that affect their lives?

Today, I bring that interdisciplinary perspective to building accessible, data-intensive digital products, with a particular interest in tools that strengthen public understanding and participation.
